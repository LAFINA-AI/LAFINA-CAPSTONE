# LAFINA v1.0.2 — Updates Inside the App

Welcome to LAFINA v1.0.2! This update changes how you get every future version of LAFINA. You no longer need to come back to GitHub, find the newest file, download it and install it again. From this version on, **LAFINA updates itself**, and tells you what's new in plain words.

---

## What's new

### LAFINA keeps itself up to date
- **It checks for you:** Every time you open LAFINA, it quietly checks whether a newer version is out. It checks again when you come back to the app later in the day.
- **It downloads on Wi-Fi by itself:** On Wi-Fi, LAFINA downloads the update in the background while you keep using the app. Nothing interrupts what you're doing.
- **It asks before using mobile data:** On mobile data, LAFINA never downloads without asking. It shows you how big the update is first, so you decide.
- **It tells you what changed:** Before you update, a short card shows the new version's name and its release notes, like the ones you're reading now.

### Most updates take a few seconds
- **Small updates:** Most updates are only a few megabytes. When it's ready, tap **Restart now** and LAFINA reopens with the new version in a couple of seconds. If you tap **Later**, the update simply applies the next time you open the app.
- **Bigger updates:** Sometimes an update changes deeper parts of the app. LAFINA downloads it, checks it, and asks you to tap **Install**. Android then shows its own confirmation, closes LAFINA for a moment, and installs the new version. A notification, *"LAFINA is installed — tap to open"*, brings you right back.
- **Interrupted downloads continue:** If your Wi-Fi drops halfway through a bigger download, LAFINA picks up where it stopped instead of starting over. If something goes wrong, tap **Try again**.

### See what's new, once
- After an update, LAFINA shows a short **What's new** card one time, so you know what changed. After that, it stays out of your way.

### Never in the way
- **No pop-ups at the wrong moment:** The update card never appears during a reminder call, a Call LAFINA conversation, the app walkthrough, or while LAFINA is asking for a permission. It waits until you're done.
- **"Later" means later:** Tap **Later** and LAFINA won't ask about that version again for a full day.
- **Check whenever you like:** Go to **Profile › About › Check for updates** at any time. The same place shows whether you're up to date.

### Safe by design
- **Only real LAFINA updates are accepted:** Every update is digitally signed by the LAFINA team. LAFINA checks that signature, and that the download is complete and untouched, before it uses anything. If something doesn't match, the update is refused.
- **A broken update undoes itself:** If a small update ever fails to start properly, LAFINA automatically goes back to the version that worked.
- **Your data stays put:** Updating never touches your tasks, notes, schedule or reminders. They stay on your phone, and your alarms keep ringing on time after an update.

### A simpler name
- On your home screen and app list, the app is now called just **LAFINA**.

## How to get this version (one time only)

This version is signed with LAFINA's new permanent release key. That's what makes automatic updates possible, but it also means Android can't install it over older test versions. **You need to do this once:**

1. **Signed in with a LAFINA account?** Open LAFINA and make sure your data has finished syncing. You'll get it back after signing in again.
2. **Using LAFINA as a guest?** Your guest data lives only on this phone and will be removed when you uninstall. Write down or copy anything you want to keep.
3. **Uninstall** the old LAFINA from your phone.
4. **Download and install** `LAFINA-v1.0.2-arm64-v8a.apk` from the files below this release. If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to install apps.
5. **Open LAFINA** and sign in (or continue as a guest).

That's it. **From now on, every update arrives inside the app.** You won't need to come back here.

---

## Good to know

- **The first bigger update** will ask you to let LAFINA install apps. Android shows a settings page: turn on **Allow from this source**, then go back. LAFINA continues the install by itself. You only do this once.
- **Updates use about 10 KB to check** and usually a few megabytes to download. Bigger updates are about 140 MB and only download by themselves on Wi-Fi.
- **LAFINA only talks to GitHub to check for updates.** It doesn't send your name, account or any of your data.
- **Phones supported:** 64-bit Android phones (almost every phone from the last several years).

## Questions

- **"I don't see an update."** You're probably already on the latest version. Check **Profile › About**: it shows your version and whether you're up to date.
- **"The update stopped downloading."** Open the update card and tap **Try again**. Bigger updates continue from where they stopped.
- **"Android says the app isn't installed."** You still have an older test version. Follow *How to get this version* above: uninstall it once, then install this one.

Thank you for testing LAFINA! Your feedback shapes every update, and now those updates reach you faster.
