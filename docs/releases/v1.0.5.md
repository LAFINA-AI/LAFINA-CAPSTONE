# LAFINA v1.0.5 — A Smarter Home, New Themes, and Faster Calls

This update refreshes your Home screen with a live countdown and daily timeline, adds new color themes and animated AI button styles, makes reminder calls faster and easier to talk to, and polishes Profile and privacy screens across the app.

## What's new

### A refreshed Home screen
- **Live Up Next countdown:** See your current or upcoming class, event, alarm, or task at a glance with a live countdown ring and time remaining.
- **One-tap Quick Actions:** Add a task, set an alarm, start a Focus timer, or jump straight into Ask USTP right from Home.
- **Live Today timeline:** Follow your day in order — finished sessions check off automatically, open gaps between classes show your free time with a quick Focus button, and upcoming items stay front and center.

### Appearance Studio: more themes & animated AI buttons
- **6 new color themes:** Try true-black **OLED**, **Midnight**, **Neon Pulse**, **Honey Gold**, **Sakura**, and **Matcha** — each tuned for both Light and Dark mode.
- **7 new AI button designs:** Pick from 6 smooth animated styles (**Pulse Wave**, **Orbital Rings**, **Aurora Breathe**, **Live Equalizer**, **Celestial Spin**, and **Quantum Sweep**) plus **Neural Mesh**. Open **Profile › Appearance › More themes** or **More designs** to preview them live.

### Profile pictures, Student setup & Privacy
- **Cleaner profile picture options:** Start with a clean neutral avatar, roll a generated character avatar, or pick a photo from your phone (photos stay privately on your phone only). You can reset back to default anytime.
- **Clearer dialogs and notices:** Redesigned **Student setup**, **Sign Out**, **Privacy Policy**, and **Chat sync** cards make every option easier to read and confirm.
- **External link notice in USTP sources:** Official USTP web links now clearly let you know when tapping them opens your browser outside LAFINA.

## What's fixed

- **Faster online reminder calls:** LAFINA now connects while reading your reminder aloud, cutting down the wait before you can talk.
- **No more repeating yourself on calls:** Saying *"Move it to 7 PM"*, *"Push it to tomorrow at 9 AM"*, or *"Call me back at 8"* during a reminder call now understands you mean that reminder, prepares one clear confirmation card (or snoozes short call-backs), and lets you revise it naturally without asking you to repeat the whole request.
- **Smarter date checks on calls:** Past or impossible dates (like November 31) now prompt you for a valid time instead of shifting to the wrong day.
- **Better Home free-time and alarm details:** Short gaps on the Today timeline no longer open an unrelated later study slot, and the Alarms & Reminders card now shows which day your next alarm rings.
- **Smoother battery behavior:** Small AI button previews inside Profile stay still when Profile is in the background, and signing out handles network hiccups cleanly.

## Good to know

- **This is a quick update.** If you're already on LAFINA v1.0.4, LAFINA downloads a small update in the background and applies it the next time you restart — no full reinstall needed. Your tasks, notes, schedule, and reminders stay safely on your phone.
- **Some Infinix, Tecno, and itel phones** may still hold back reminders while the screen is off. Keep LAFINA's battery setting on **Unrestricted** (Settings › Apps › LAFINA › Battery).
