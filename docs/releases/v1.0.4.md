# LAFINA v1.0.4 — Ask USTP and a Friendlier Profile

This update turns the USTP Handbook chat into **Ask USTP**, makes Profile easier to find your way around, and fixes problems with calendars, reminders, recordings and accounts.

## What's new

### Ask USTP
The USTP Handbook chat is now called **Ask USTP**, and it knows a lot more.
- **Ask about USTP Cagayan de Oro, not just handbook rules:**
  - semester, exam and enrollment dates for AY 2026-2027
  - offices and contact numbers
  - PRISMS and the other student portals
  - the library
  - programs offered
  - scholarships and student organizations

  It works without internet.
- **See where every answer came from.** Each answer lists the official USTP pages it used, and the academic calendar page number. Tap a link to open the page.
- **Read the source yourself.** Tap **See passages** under an answer to read the exact text LAFINA answered from.
- **Browse everything.** Open **USTP sources** in the chat menu to look through or search everything Ask USTP knows.
- **No guessing when USTP's own pages disagree.** The 2027 midyear start date, for example, is printed two different ways in the official calendar. LAFINA shows both dates and asks you to check with the Registrar.
- **Dates you can trust to be current.** The campus information was checked on November 24, 2026. LAFINA tells you when it may be out of date.

Ask USTP is available after you add your USTP COR.

### A friendlier Profile
- **Settings are grouped by what you want to do:** Reminders & schedule, Assistant, Voice, School, Appearance, and About & help. Shortcuts at the top take you straight to each group.
- **Plain names.** For example, "Alarm & Reminder Health" is now **Check my reminders work**. Each setting shows its current choice under its name.
- **A new "What LAFINA remembers" page.** See your saved memories as cards, pin the important ones, edit them, or forget them.
- **Clearer cards during calls with LAFINA.** Titles are in bold, and dates and times read like "Wed, Nov 25 · 2:00 PM".
- **Shorter, plainer wording** all through the app.

### Adding your COR
- **Missing class dates are easy to spot.** If you forget your first or last class date, LAFINA now shows what's missing and scrolls to it, instead of doing nothing.
- **One-tap dates.** LAFINA offers USTP's official 1st semester dates, August 10 to December 19, 2026. Nothing is filled in until you tap.

## What's fixed

- **Signing out stops a recording.** A meeting recording now stops as soon as you sign out.
- **Clear All Data clears everything.** It also removes recordings and files saved on your phone. If a file can't be removed, LAFINA tells you and tries again later.
- **Imported calendars keep the right times.** Calendars imported from other apps no longer move events by 8 hours.
- **Repeating reminders stop when they should.** A reminder set to repeat a number of times now stops after the last one. Monthly reminders near the end of the month stay on the right day.
- **Older repeating events stay put.** They no longer disappear from your calendar.
- **Completed stays completed.** Finished tasks in an imported calendar stay finished.
- **Deleted chats are deleted everywhere.** A chat you delete while chat sync is off is now also deleted from your account.
- **Big calendars don't slow LAFINA down.** Exporting a big calendar no longer slows the app, and an oversized calendar file is turned away instead of freezing it.
- **Switching accounts is safer.** LAFINA no longer finishes a request, a study job or a call from the account you just signed out of.
- **Replies stay in the right chat.** A slow reply no longer appears after you delete that chat or clear your history.
- **Calls without internet end on time.** A call with LAFINA now ends at its time limit, even when you've muted it.
- **Deleting a GWA semester deletes it.** Its grades no longer come back when you open the GWA calculator again.
- **Pausing Pomodoro sticks.** Pausing or resetting the timer while Android asks about notifications no longer starts it again.

## Good to know

- **Some Infinix, Tecno and itel phones** may hold back reminders while the screen is off and deliver them when you unlock. We're still working on this. For now, keep LAFINA's battery setting on **Unrestricted** (Settings › Apps › LAFINA › Battery).
- **This is a bigger update.** LAFINA downloads it by itself on Wi-Fi, then asks you to tap **Install**. Your tasks, notes, schedule and reminders stay on your phone.
