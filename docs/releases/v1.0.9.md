# LAFINA v1.0.9 — Built-In USTP Academic Calendar & Smoother Call Edits

This update brings the official **USTP Academic Calendar (AY 2026–2027)** right into your Calendar tab, makes changing schedule plans mid-call feel effortless, and helps LAFINA understand everyday USTP Handbook questions even more reliably during voice calls.

## What's new

### Built-in USTP Academic Calendar (AY 2026–2027)
- **Key university dates right on your calendar:** Confirmed USTP students now see **1st Semester**, **2nd Semester**, and **Midyear 2027** exam schedules, enrollment periods, term milestones, campus events, admissions dates, and major deadlines across Day, Week, and Month views — even offline.
- **Browse by term & jump to any date:** Open the Calendar menu and tap **USTP Academic Calendar** to filter by **All**, **1st Sem**, **2nd Sem**, or **Midyear**, then tap any event to jump straight to that day.
- **Clean & customizable:** Toggle **USTP Academic Calendar** on or off anytime from the Calendar menu or Layers, and optionally turn on **Graduate School** dates if you need them (off by default). Long multi-month windows appear cleanly as start and deadline markers without cluttering every day in between, national Philippine holidays aren't duplicated, and any dates that need Registrar confirmation are clearly noted.

## What's improved & fixed

- **Smoother schedule changes during calls & Chat:** If you ask LAFINA to schedule something during a call (like *"7 PM to 8 PM"*) and adjust it on the spot (*"Make it 7:30 to 8"*, *"Change the end time to 8:30"*, or *"Push it to 8 PM"*), LAFINA now updates the same confirmation card smoothly without losing track or creating duplicates — and saves it cleanly as soon as you say *"Yes, save it"* or tap **Confirm**.
- **On-screen edits stay in sync on calls:** Editing a confirmation card by hand during a voice call immediately keeps LAFINA up to date, and polite revisions in Chat (*"Can you change it to 7:30 PM instead?"*, *"Yes, please save it"*) work naturally.
- **Better USTP Handbook answers in Voice Calls:** Asking about dress code and campus rules in natural everyday wording or Taglish/Bisaya — like *"Is it okay to use tattered jeans?"*, *"Can students wear ripped pants?"*, *"Are earrings or tube tops allowed?"*, or *"Ok ra ba mag tattered jeans?"* — now finds the exact USTP Student Handbook rule more consistently.

## Good to know

- **This is a quick update.** If you're already on LAFINA v1.0.8, LAFINA downloads a small update (just a few MB) and applies it with a quick restart — no full reinstall needed. If you're updating from v1.0.7 or earlier, LAFINA will download the full app update automatically. Your tasks, notes, schedule, memories, and reminders stay safely on your phone.
- **USTP Academic Calendar** appears automatically in your Calendar once your USTP COR is confirmed.
