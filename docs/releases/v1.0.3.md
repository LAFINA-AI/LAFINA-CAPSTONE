# LAFINA v1.0.3 — Calls and Chat Fixes

This update fixes problems we found while testing LAFINA on a real phone. Reminder calls, chat and quick reminders all behave better now.

## What's fixed

### Reminder calls
- **The call screen now opens while you're using LAFINA.** Before, if LAFINA was open when a reminder call was due, you only got a small notification at the top. Now the full call screen appears right away so you can answer or decline.

### Chat
- **Chat follows the conversation.** New replies and review cards no longer hide under the message box. The newest message stays in view. If you scrolled up to read something older, LAFINA leaves you where you are.
- **Better understanding of short requests.** Questions like "Explain photosynthesis in one sentence" now get a real answer instead of "Which one do you mean?".
- **Your titles keep their spelling.** Reminders and tasks saved from online chat keep "LAFINA" and other words exactly as you typed them.

### Quick reminders
- **"In 1 minute" really means at least a minute.** Short reminders like "remind me in 2 minutes" are now set for the next full minute after the time you asked for. Before, they could land a few seconds early and expire before you pressed Confirm.

## Good to know

- **Some Infinix, Tecno and itel phones** may hold back reminders while the screen is off and deliver them when you unlock. We're working on a fix. For now, keep LAFINA's battery setting on **Unrestricted** (Settings › Apps › LAFINA › Battery).
- This is a bigger update (about 140 MB). LAFINA downloads it by itself on Wi-Fi, then asks you to tap **Install**. Your tasks, notes, schedule and reminders stay on your phone.
