# LAFINA v1.0.6 — Reliability, Alarms & Sync Hotfix

This hotfix strengthens the everyday features you rely on most — making alarms, reminder calls, cloud sync, Ask USTP, and your Home screen faster, steadier, and more dependable.

## What's fixed

### Alarms, Morning Briefing & Reminder Calls
- **Alarms that ring on time without doubling up:** Fixed edge cases where repeating alarms or snoozes could fire twice, play too softly, or hold onto your phone's audio after you dismiss them.
- **Cleaner Morning Briefing:** Stopping or dismissing your Morning Briefing now silences playback right away and hands audio back to your phone cleanly.
- **Steadier reminder calls:** If your internet drops mid-call, LAFINA switches smoothly to on-device voice and closes the call screen cleanly when you're done instead of freezing.

### Accounts, Cloud Sync & Your Data
- **Safer Guest-to-Account upgrade:** Signing in or creating an account after using Guest mode now carries over your tasks, notes, schedule, and chats without losing or duplicating items.
- **More reliable Cloud & Chat sync:** Fixed rare sync hiccups where offline edits or cleared chats could reappear after reconnecting, and made Sign Out and **Clear All Data** remove leftover alarms and files thoroughly.
- **Accurate recurring schedules & calendar imports:** Repeating classes, events, and imported calendar files now stay on the right days without drifting across months or time zones.

### Chat, Ask USTP & Everyday Tools
- **Better Ask USTP & Chat Undo:** Improved USTP Student Handbook lookups, fixed multi-step Undo in Chat so reverting an action restores everything cleanly, and ensured Temporary Chat stays private without saving memories.
- **Accurate Home screen & COR schedule scan:** Overnight study blocks and category filters on the Home screen now show the right items, and undoing a COR class-schedule scan removes imported classes cleanly.
- **Pomodoro Focus & Floating LAFINA polish:** Focus Silence now turns off reliably when a timer finishes or is cancelled, and Floating LAFINA and document tools clean up smoothly in the background.

## Good to know

- **This is a full app update.** Because v1.0.6 includes under-the-hood Android improvements for alarms, calls, and scanning, LAFINA will download the updated app and Android will ask you to tap **Install**. Your tasks, notes, schedule, and reminders stay safely on your phone.
- **Some Infinix, Tecno, and itel phones** may still hold back reminders while the screen is off. Keep LAFINA's battery setting on **Unrestricted** (**Settings › Apps › LAFINA › Battery**).
