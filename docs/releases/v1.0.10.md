# LAFINA v1.0.10 — Steadier Calls, Smarter Chat & Cleaner Schedule Tools

This update makes **Call LAFINA**, **Reminder Calls**, and **Chat** feel noticeably smoother and more dependable in everyday student life — from steadier voice connections and smarter lock-screen calls to cleaner USTP source previews, clearer GWA subject names, and simpler default event lengths.

## What's improved & fixed

### Steadier Calls & Lock-Screen Reminders
- **Calls bounce back from brief signal drops:** Short Wi-Fi or mobile data hiccups no longer cut your call short or falsely sign you out. You can also type a message while LAFINA is still speaking and hear her answer right away.
- **Automatic headset & Bluetooth switching:** Plugging in or connecting earbuds mid-call switches audio cleanly without getting stuck on the earpiece, and alarms now pause voice calls cleanly instead of ringing over them.
- **Calmer lock-screen reminder calls:** Tapping **Answer** on the lock screen silences the ringtone immediately while LAFINA opens, rotating your phone no longer interrupts the ringing screen, reopening LAFINA from Recents won't replay finished calls, and unread reminder notifications stay in your shade when you return to the app.
- **More natural voice replies:** Phrases like *"Call me back in 10 minutes"*, *"Move it to 7"*, *"Snooze 5"*, and *"No, later"* work reliably on reminder calls.
- **Add two reminders in one breath:** Asking something like *"Remind me to go walking tomorrow at 7 AM and then on Monday 8 AM to go jogging"* now lines up both items cleanly on a single review card, and saying *"Save both"* confirms them together.

### Better USTP Handbook Answers & GWA Names
- **Compact USTP source cards in calls:** When LAFINA looks up USTP Student Handbook or campus rules during a call, you'll see a clean preview card with the matching passage, page, and a quick **See passages** button — without awkward blank gaps or stale results carrying over from earlier questions.
- **Natural phrasing works even better:** Everyday questions like *"What's the dress code?"* or *"Why can't I wear slippers?"* find the right handbook answer more consistently.
- **Real subject names in GWA:** Importing your COR into the GWA calculator now displays full subject names instead of cryptic course codes, and updates existing rows cleanly without creating duplicates.

### Smarter Chat, Quick Chat & Schedule Settings
- **Cleaner Chat & Quick Chat experience:** Follow-up questions about a different semester or academic year keep dates accurate, editing a task card lets you clear a due date or time easily, and Quick Chat stays neatly sized and in sync when your connection changes.
- **Simpler default event duration:** Set your preferred default length for new events (15–480 minutes, default 60) right inside **Profile › Preferences › Schedule**, and LAFINA will ask when an event should start instead of guessing.

## Good to know

- **This is a full app update.** Because v1.0.10 includes improvements to Android call audio and lock-screen reminders, LAFINA will download the updated app package and Android will ask you to confirm **Install**. All of your tasks, notes, schedule, memories, and reminders stay safely on your phone.
