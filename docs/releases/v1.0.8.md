# LAFINA v1.0.8 — USTP Handbook in Calls, Auto-Reconnecting Calls & Quick Chat History

This update lets you ask about the USTP Student Handbook and campus info right during voice calls, keeps online calls going through brief signal drops, adds conversation history to Floating Quick Chat, and delivers a big round of speed and reliability improvements across the app.

## What's new

### Ask about USTP rules & campus info in Voice Calls
- **Handbook answers aloud — online or offline:** Ask natural questions during any Call LAFINA or reminder call — like *"Is it okay to wear ripped jeans on campus?"*, *"What's the rule on hair color?"*, or *"When are second semester midterms?"* — and LAFINA checks your on-device USTP Student Handbook and USTP CDO info right away.
- **Tap to verify the exact source:** During a call, a dismissible **USTP Sources** card appears showing the exact section and page number. Tap **See passages** anytime to read the original handbook text, and LAFINA saves the citations to your Chat history afterward.

### Calls that bounce back & remember your chat
- **Automatic call reconnect:** If your Wi-Fi or mobile data briefly drops during an online call, LAFINA now shows **Reconnecting…** and automatically restores your call instead of cutting you off.
- **Calls remember your recent Chat:** Starting a voice call from Chat now shares your recent conversation context with LAFINA, so you can pick up right where you left off without repeating yourself.

### Floating Quick Chat History & smoother follow-ups
- **Quick Chat History:** Browse your recent Floating Quick Chat conversations and clear them whenever you want with a simple confirmation.
- **Natural multi-step follow-ups:** When you ask LAFINA to add a task or reminder and leave out a detail (like the time), you can reply naturally in the next message and LAFINA will pick it right up.

## What's improved & fixed

- **Faster & smoother everywhere:** Optimized database lookups, paused background animations to save battery, and sped up saving changes during voice calls.
- **More reliable alarms, calls & sync:** Strengthened alarm delivery, reminder call stability, calendar file imports, offline voice processing, meeting recording safety, and cloud sync conflict handling.
- **Cleaner Skills & Meeting Notes:** Polished the All Skills view and improved on-device meeting transcription defaults.

## Good to know

- **This is a full app update.** Because v1.0.8 includes native Android improvements for call reconnection and stability, LAFINA will download the updated app and Android will ask you to tap **Install**. Your tasks, notes, schedule, memories, and reminders stay safely on your phone.
- **USTP Handbook in calls** works automatically once your USTP COR is confirmed and USTP Handbook is turned on in your settings.
