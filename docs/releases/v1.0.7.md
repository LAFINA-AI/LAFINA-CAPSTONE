# LAFINA v1.0.7 — Lock-Screen Reminder Calls, Custom Home Studio & Smarter Memory

This update fixes reminder calls when your phone is locked, brings a new Customize Studio to your Home screen, and makes LAFINA's memory smarter, safer, and easier to manage across Chat, Quick Chat, and Voice.

## What's new

### Make Home yours
- **4 Up Next card styles & 3 finishes:** Choose between **Countdown Ring**, **Mascot & Progress**, **Orb & Brief**, and **Compact**, paired with **Vivid**, **Soft Tint**, or **Minimal** finishes — all tuned for crisp readability across all 12 Light and Dark themes.
- **Customizable Quick Actions & layout:** Pick your favorite 2–4 Home shortcuts, switch between **Comfortable** and **Compact** spacing, and toggle your greeting avatar anytime from **Customize Dashboard** (or long-press the Up Next card).

### Smarter, safer Memory
- **Natural preference capture:** Tell LAFINA how you study (*"I'm a night owl, I prefer studying at night till 1am"*), your preferred name, or your academic goals in plain words — LAFINA saves them to Memory (never misfiling them as notes or tasks) and uses them automatically for study scheduling and Morning Briefings.
- **Ask and forget naturally — with Undo everywhere:** Ask *"What do you remember about me?"*, *"What's my sleep schedule?"*, or *"Forget that I'm a morning person"* in Chat, Quick Chat, Temporary Chat, or Voice calls, with one-tap **Undo** if you change your mind.
- **Built-in privacy guard:** Passwords, PINs, OTPs, payment cards, and bank or GCash numbers are automatically blocked from being saved to Memory.

## What's fixed

- **Reminder calls now show over the lock screen:** When your phone is asleep and locked, incoming reminder calls now appear right on top of your lock screen instead of staying hidden behind it. Tap **Answer** to unlock straight into LAFINA, or **Decline** to snooze without unlocking — even offline.
- **Home tab reliability & polish:** Fixed recurring tasks failing to check off from Home, accurate free-time gaps when filtering Today's timeline, instant Home updates after changing Preferences, accurate Pomodoro countdowns, cleaner overnight schedule labels, and easier-to-tap task checkboxes.
- **Smarter study scheduling & sync:** Fixed late-night study scheduling so blocks roll cleanly to tomorrow instead of proposing past morning times, kept your preferred name and goals synced across Chat, Profile, and Guest-to-Account upgrades, and ensured regular questions (*"Do you know Python?"* or *"Forget my 3pm alarm"*) are never confused with Memory commands.

## Good to know

- **This is a full app update.** Because v1.0.7 includes the native Android lock-screen reminder call fix, LAFINA will download the updated app and Android will ask you to tap **Install**. Your tasks, notes, schedule, memories, and reminders stay safely on your phone.
- **Some Infinix, Tecno, and itel phones** may still hold back reminders while the screen is off. Keep LAFINA's battery setting on **Unrestricted** (**Settings › Apps › LAFINA › Battery**).
