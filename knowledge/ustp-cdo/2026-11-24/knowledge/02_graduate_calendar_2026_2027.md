# Graduate (Gr) events in official PDF

The source contains side-by-side undergraduate and graduate calendars. It is important not to apply graduate events to a BSIT or other undergraduate student. The PDF page 2 term summary is repeated with an **"UNDERGRADUATE (UG)"** heading, so do **not** confidently claim a separate graduate term summary is specified. Use the dated graduate event rows below instead.
