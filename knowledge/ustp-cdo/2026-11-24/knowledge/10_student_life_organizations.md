# Campus life

USTP Cagayan de Oro maintains student organizations, councils, campus activities and student support services. The official Week of Welcome site has a **historical directory** of curricular and non-curricular groups. Its footer identifies the site as **Virtual Week of Welcome 2021**, so the names here establish groups that the university has publicly listed, **not verified active membership or officers in AY 2026-2027**.

# Selected curricular organizations in the historic directory

- **Student Council of Information Technology and Computing (SCITC)**: student council of CITC.
- **Society of Information Technology Enthusiasts (SITE)**: listed among computing organizations.
- **Guild of Junior Data Scientists**.
- **State University Technology Communication Management Society**.
- **Student Council of Engineering and Architecture**.
- **Student Council of Science and Mathematics**.
- **Student Council of Science and Technology Education**.
- **Student Council of Technology**.
- **Institute of Computer Engineers of the Philippines** student organization.
- **Junior Philippine Institute of Civil Engineers**, **Junior Philippine Society of Mechanical Engineers**, and other engineering groups.

Source: https://www.ustp.edu.ph/week-of-welcome/organizations/cagayan-de-oro/cdo-curricular/.

# Selected noncurricular organizations in the historic directory

- **USTP Scholars' Society**: historical site says this is a federated student organization for scholarship grantees and a link to ASO.
- **USTP DOST-SEI Scholars Guild** and **Association of CHED-TES Scholars** (named by site).
- **The Trailblazer Publication**: described as the official student publication of USTP CDO.
- **USTP Chess Enthusiasts**.
- **Sanghimig Chorale**, **Playmakers**, **Gintong Amihan Dance Troupe** and other arts/culture groups.
- **Google Developer Student Clubs USTP**, the name displayed on this historical page; **do not imply this remains the current chapter brand**.

Source: https://www.ustp.edu.ph/week-of-welcome/organizations/cagayan-de-oro/cdo-non-curricular/.

# Practical guidance

For **current** recognized organizations, open registration, officer rosters, organization rooms, events and the current SITE/SCITC leadership, ask the Student Affairs Office and check official 2026 campus announcements. Do not use historic signup forms from the virtual WoW page as the default join link.
