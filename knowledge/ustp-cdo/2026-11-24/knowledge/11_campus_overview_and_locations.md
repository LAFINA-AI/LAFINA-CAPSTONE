# What is USTP?

The **University of Science and Technology of Southern Philippines (USTP)** is a Philippine public/state university system. The university website states it was established **August 16, 2016**, under **Republic Act 10919**, by amalgamating the former **Mindanao University of Science and Technology (MUST)** in Cagayan de Oro and **Misamis Oriental State College of Agriculture and Technology (MOSCAT)** in Claveria.

Official general history: https://www.ustp.edu.ph/.

# USTP Cagayan de Oro campus

- Known as **USTP Cagayan de Oro**, **USTP CDO**, or **USTP CdeO**.
- **Address:** Claro M. Recto Avenue, Lapasan, Cagayan de Oro City 9000, Philippines.
- Classified as a **major campus** of the USTP system, not the same site as USTP Balubal or USTP Alubijid.
- Official campus profile states an approximate land area of **9 hectares**.
- University states CDO was CHED-recognized **Center of Excellence in Information Technology** and **Centers of Development in Electrical Engineering and Mathematics**. These are historical/recognition descriptions on the university profile; confirm current status with CHED when time-sensitive.
- USTP's site features an official **360-degree virtual tour** link on its campus profile, but this knowledge pack contains no verified GPS coordinates for individual indoor rooms.

Source: https://www.ustp.edu.ph/campuses/ and https://www.ustp.edu.ph/cdeo/.

# Do not confuse campuses

The USTP campus page identifies **Alubijid** as the university's main campus. **Cagayan de Oro and Claveria** are major campuses; the same page also calls **Villanueva** a major campus. **Balubal**, while within Cagayan de Oro City, is a **distinct USTP campus** and not interchangeable with the C.M. Recto campus. **Jasaan, Oroquieta and Panaon** are also distinct. Programs, offices and calendars may apply differently per campus.

The CHED calendar letter covers **Alubijid, Balubal, Cagayan de Oro, Claveria, Jasaan, Oroquieta, Panaon, Villanueva**. Its campus coverage does not mean every program or service operates identically on all sites.
