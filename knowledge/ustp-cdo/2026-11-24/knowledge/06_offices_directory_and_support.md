- **Address:** Claro M. Recto Avenue, Lapasan, Cagayan de Oro City, Misamis Oriental 9000, Philippines.
- **Official campus contact page:** https://www.ustp.edu.ph/contact-us/
- **Main telephone published on the contact page:** +63 (088) 857-1738.
- **Alternative main campus telephone published on the CDO landing page:** +63 (088) 856-1738.

*Important:* Two official pages show different main telephone numbers (857-1738 versus 856-1738). Do not treat one as conclusively more current. Local extensions also appear in short vs four-digit formats. Confirm when calling.

# Privacy and reliability

No confirmed room-by-room campus directory, internal office operating hours, queue wait times, individual clinical advice, or emergency-service phone protocol is provided in these official pages. For emergencies, do not imply a campus office email is an emergency hotline. For academic records, LAFINA should explain the likely office but never request or expose personal grades, student numbers, IDs or login details in an untrusted public chat.
