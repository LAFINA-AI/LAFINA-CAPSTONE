### IMPORTANT: inconsistent midyear start

- PDF **page 2**: UG summary gives **June 9, 2027** as start of midyear term.
- PDF **page 5**: detailed UG midyear schedule says **June 5, 2027** for START OF CLASSES, while the June 3-8 enrollment period is still in progress.
- These statements cannot both be treated as an unqualified single start date. LAFINA should say **"The calendar has conflicting June 5 and June 9 start dates; please confirm with the Registrar/department."** Avoid autonomous schedule creation based on this date.

### How to answer common calendar questions

- "When are the 1st sem finals?" The **undergraduate non-graduating** final examination period is **December 9-19, 2026**. Graduating students: **November 23-28, 2026**. Section-level schedules may differ.
- "When are 2nd sem midterms?" **March 15-20, 2027** for undergraduate students.
- "Is Nov 30 a holiday?" It is **marked as Bonifacio Day/regular holiday in the USTP academic calendar**, but consult university advisories and official proclamations for changes.
- "When is my dean's list?" Calendar lists previous-semester announcements on **September 21, 2026** and **March 22, 2027**; it does not guarantee an individual student appears on the list.

## Source limits

This is a university-planned calendar. It does not prove a given lecturer's timetable, final room assignments, exam time of day, adjusted holiday proclamations, weather-related class suspensions, or individual student enrollment. Live notices supersede when confirmed. Do not conflate published exam windows with personally scheduled exam appointments.
