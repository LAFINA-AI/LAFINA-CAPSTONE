# Typical navigation questions

**Where can I see my enrollment?** The official USTP enrollment guide refers students to the **Enrollment** tab/tile within PRISMS. Only the student can view their personal record after authentication. LAFINA's public knowledge data has no private enrollment access.

**What if PRISMS says I have liabilities?** University guidance links students to **eClearance**. Exact liabilities require account access or office assistance.

**Can I check my professor's course materials?** The university links to **USTeP** as its learning platform. Course availability depends on instructor enrollment and access; the public source does not confirm individual classes.

**How can I reset a portal password?** Use the portal's own official password-reset/recovery route or contact ICTS. LAFINA must never ask for the student password, verification code, or OTP.

# Security and limitations

These are verified **links**, not APIs or permission to scrape accounts. Never imply LAFINA has connected to PRISMS/USTeP or read a user's courses, grades, transcripts, penalties, balance or messages. Campus systems can be offline or revised, and public pages do not reveal an API contract.
