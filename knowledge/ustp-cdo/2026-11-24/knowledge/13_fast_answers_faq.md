# Fast answers with cited source boundaries

**Q: When does first semester AY 2026-2027 end?**  
A: **December 19, 2026** according to the undergraduate term summary in the official calendar (PDF page 2). The non-graduating undergraduate final exam window is **December 9-19** (PDF page 3).

**Q: When does second semester start?**  
A: **January 5, 2027** is the second-semester class start in the published university calendar (PDF pages 2 and 4).

**Q: When do I enroll for the second semester?**  
A: The calendar lists **January 4-9, 2027** for undergraduate enrollment and **January 6-9** for graduate enrollment (PDF pages 4-5). Actual section opening and PRISMS access may vary.

**Q: When are undergraduate midterms?**  
A: **October 5-10, 2026** for first semester; **March 15-20, 2027** for second semester (PDF pages 3-4).

**Q: When is USTP Week of Welcome?**  
A: **August 3-8, 2026** is listed for AY 2026-2027 (PDF page 2).

**Q: When is our midyear start?**  
A: The official 2027 PDF is inconsistent: its undergraduate term summary says **June 9**, but the detailed undergraduate row says **June 5**. Confirm with USTP CDO Registrar before planning travel or schedules (PDF pp. 2, 5).

**Q: Where can I check PRISMS?**  
A: https://prisms.ustp.edu.ph/ (university student system); LAFINA cannot see individual student grades/enrollment without an authorized integration.

**Q: Where do I upload admissions info?**  
A: Use the official admissions portal https://prisms.ustp.edu.ph/padmission and current [ASO information](https://www.ustp.edu.ph/cdeo/admission-and-scholarship-office/). Never put login credentials into LAFINA chat.

**Q: Is tuition free?**  
A: Qualified students may be covered under **RA 10931** as described by the university. This does not guarantee every fee or student is covered. Verify eligibility with admissions/cashier.

**Q: Where is the Main Library?**  
A: **3rd floor of the Learning Resource Center (LRC) building** according to the CDO Library page.

**Q: Where is the e-Library?**  
A: **2nd floor, left wing, LRC building**, per official CDO Library page.

**Q: How can I ask about transcripts or enrollment records?**  
A: Contact the **Registrar's Office** using the published CDO main-line extension **137** on the official contacts page. Exact processing fees and timelines are not verified here.

**Q: Does USTP CDO offer BSIT or BSCS?**  
A: Both appear in the official CDO undergraduate program list, but the list alone cannot confirm the program is taking applications this cycle.

**Q: Can you tell me my GPA, enrollment subjects, class section or clearance?**  
A: Not from public USTP data. Personal information requires separate authenticated and authorized access.

**Q: When will exams be cancelled due to typhoon?**  
A: A dated university advisory or local authority announcement is required. The archived annual calendar does **not** itself establish live suspensions.

**Q: Can you make a reminder for me?**  
A: Only after the student requests it. Calendar dates are from the published university calendar and must be labeled as scheduled, not guaranteed; conflicted midyear dates should be verified first.
