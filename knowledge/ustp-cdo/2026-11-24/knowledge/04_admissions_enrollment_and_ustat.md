# USTAT

USTAT is the *University of Science and Technology Admission Test*. USTP CDO's ASO website describes both **home-based** and **onsite** USTAT activities. Details of which options are available in a particular cycle must be checked in current admissions announcements. No exact 2027 test date is established in this knowledge pack.

# Pre-enrollment / PRISMS guide as described on USTP's official site

1. After receiving a USTP application email, verify your email through the official verification link.
2. Use the student number/username and temporary credentials provided by the university to access PRISMS; change the temporary password.
3. Open the **Enrollment** area in PRISMS.
4. Complete missing profile information if the portal prompts you.
5. If the portal reports outstanding liabilities, follow the official eClearance route: https://clearance.ustp.edu.ph/.
6. Select the offered class section; subjects associated with it will appear. Tick the desired subjects, register them, and proceed to confirmation.
7. Review the enrollment details, save/continue, and print the **pre-assessment** for validation; USTP says a confirmation email may be sent.
8. The official instructions distinguish **online pre-enrollment** from full validation/enrollment. Do not claim pre-enrollment alone makes a student officially enrolled.

Source: https://www.ustp.edu.ph/enrollment-flow/ (official guide; steps may change with PRISMS updates).

# Tuition/RA 10931

The official university admissions page states eligible/qualified undergraduate students benefit from public funding under **Republic Act 10931** (Universal Access to Quality Tertiary Education Act). It does **not** follow that every student is automatically eligible or that every fee for every level/circumstance is waived. Refer individual eligibility and charges to ASO/Registrar/cashier. A fee information page exists at https://www.ustp.edu.ph/tuition-fees/ but exact amounts in a linked external fee sheet were not verified here.

# Scope and cautions

Do not claim admission slots are open simply because a general admissions portal loads. Do not fabricate passing scores, cutoffs, eligibility exceptions, program-specific requirements, document checklists, application deadlines, current fees, or 'USTAT guaranteed admission'. Do not solicit a student's temporary password, OTP or account recovery tokens. The older official USTAT results page mixes past school years and is unsuitable for live 2027 schedules.
