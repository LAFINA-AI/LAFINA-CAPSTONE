# Library catalog and main services

- **OPAC (Online Public Access Catalog)** is described as a way to locate books, check listed availability and identify call numbers.
- Reader services include circulation, reference, reserve, periodicals, Filipiniana and special collections.
- Undergraduate students may borrow **up to three circulation books for seven days** with a listed **seven-day renewal period**, according to the published Library Services page. Verify current lending restrictions before depending on these terms.
- Official page lists **PHP 5 per day** penalty for overdue general borrowing and a separate **PHP 5 per hour** for overdue special overnight reserve loans. These are website-published policies, not a live debt assessment.
- Reservation of a campus audio-visual room is described as: check availability; request an AVR reservation form at Main Library; obtain departmental/instructor endorsement; return completed form.

## Audio-visual rooms (published locations)

- AVR 1: **Ground floor, ITB Phase 2**.
- AVR 2: **4th floor, ICT Building**.
- AVR 3: **6th floor, Engineering Complex**.

These locations and the reservation outline appear on the official [Library Services page](https://www.ustp.edu.ph/cdeo/library/services/). Don't promise the rooms are free at a specified date/time.
