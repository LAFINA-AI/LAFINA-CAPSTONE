# Scholarship types listed on USTP CDO site

The campus scholarship page names **institutionally funded** awards associated with BOR Resolution No. 97, series 2019, including:

- Entrance Scholarship
- Academic Excellence
- Leadership Scholarship
- Grant-in-Aid for Minority
- ARCU Scholarship
- Athletic Scholarship
- NSTP-ROTC Scholarship

The page also mentions third-party programs and providers, including CHED grant-in-aids, DOST-SEI, CEPALCO, ICTSI, NAPOLCOM, government insurance and industry/private foundations. This is an **overview of scholarship types and named partners**, not a current application list.

# What LAFINA should and should not say

- **Can say:** "USTP CDO lists entrance, academic excellence, leadership, athletic and other scholarships on its official scholarship page. You can contact ASO to check which applications are accepting students now."
- **Cannot claim without further verification:** that a particular program is active in November 2026, the current stipend or award amount, income qualification, grade threshold, application documents, slot availability, release date, or that every student receives an allowance.
- **Do not conflate:** RA 10931's eligibility-based free tuition support with CHED TES (Tertiary Education Subsidy) or other scholarship grants.
